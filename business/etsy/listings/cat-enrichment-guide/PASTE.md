A bored indoor cat is a restless cat. Give yours 30 easy, low-cost ways to hunt, climb, explore and play.

Indoor cats live safer lives, but they still have the instincts of a hunter. This guide gives you practical, mostly DIY enrichment ideas using things you already have at home, plus a simple 4-week plan so enrichment becomes part of your routine instead of an afterthought.

WHAT'S INSIDE (12 pages, US Letter PDF)
- Play styles and the prey sequence: how to play like prey with wand toys
- 30 DIY enrichment ideas: box castles, crinkle balls, treat hunts, paper bag caves and more
- Vertical space and environment: scratching, hiding spots and a resource setup checklist
- Food puzzles and foraging, with a notes page for what your cat likes
- Clicker training basics for cats and first tricks
- Multi-cat tips and signs of boredom or stress (play or fight?)
- 4-week enrichment calendar: one featured idea per day with session check boxes
- Printable play log to track what your cat loves (and ignores)
- Safety notes on strings, small parts and toy supervision

WHO IT'S FOR
- Indoor cat owners whose cats seem bored, overactive at night or under-stimulated
- Apartment dwellers with limited space
- Owners of new kittens or newly adopted cats
- Multi-cat homes looking to reduce tension through play
