Bringing home a new dog or cat? Get everything ready the first time, without overbuying or forgetting the essentials.

The days before and after a new pet arrives are exciting and overwhelming. This printable kit turns the to-do list in your head into simple checklists, so you can shop smart, pet-proof your home and settle in with confidence.

WHAT'S INSIDE (12 pages, US Letter PDF)
- Pre-adoption questions to ask the shelter, rescue or breeder
- Dog shopping checklist: essentials vs. nice-to-haves, plus space for your extras
- Cat shopping checklist: litter setup, scratching, feeding, play
- Home pet-proofing checklist, room by room: cords, plants, chemicals, small objects
- First 24 hours, first week and first month plan: homecoming, sleep, feeding, decompression, routines
- First vet visit question list and what to bring
- Budget planner worksheet: one-time setup costs, monthly costs, annual and emergency planning
- Emergency contacts sheet for sitters, family members and backup caregivers

WHO IT'S FOR
- First-time dog or cat owners preparing for adoption day
- Families adding a second pet who want a fresh, organized plan
- Shelter and rescue adopters who want a calm, low-stress transition
- Gift-givers helping a friend welcome a new pet
