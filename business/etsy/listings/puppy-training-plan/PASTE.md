Turn puppy chaos into calm routines in 30 days, with 3 to 5 minute training sessions that actually fit your life.

Most new puppy owners do not need more tips. They need a plan: what to teach today, what to practice tomorrow, and how to know it is working. This week-by-week, positive-reinforcement program takes you from day one to a confident, well-mannered pup, one small win at a time.

WHAT'S INSIDE (19 pages, US Letter PDF)
- Week 1: Foundations - marker word, name recognition, handling, crate comfort
- Week 2: Sit, down and gentle mouths
- Week 3: Stay and come (recall)
- Week 4: Leash walking and real-life manners
- Potty training schedule with "how long can my puppy hold it" chart
- Crate training step by step
- Bite inhibition and nipping four-part plan
- Socialization checklist and new-experience log
- The 30-Day Daily Plan table: exactly what to practice each day, with tick boxes
- Troubleshooting FAQ: chewing, jumping, crate whining, accidents
- Printable progress tracker, weekly reflections and a daily potty log

WHO IT'S FOR
- New puppy owners (roughly 8 weeks to 6 months) who want a clear starting point
- Busy families who can commit 10 to 15 minutes a day, split into short sessions
- Anyone who prefers kind, reward-based training over harsh corrections
- Adopters of young rescue dogs building trust and routine from scratch
