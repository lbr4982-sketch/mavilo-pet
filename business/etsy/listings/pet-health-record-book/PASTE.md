Never dig through emails for vaccine dates again. Keep your pet's whole health story in one organized place.

When the boarding kennel asks for proof of rabies vaccination, or the emergency vet asks when the last dose was given, you will have the answer in seconds. This printable record book keeps vaccinations, vet visits, medications, weight and grooming neatly logged for every pet in your home.

WHAT'S INSIDE (14 pages, US Letter PDF)
- Pet profile: photo, microchip number, insurance, vet and emergency contacts
- Vaccination log: vaccine, date given, lot number, next due date
- Vet visit log: reason, findings, recommendations, follow-ups
- Medication log for prescriptions, supplements and topical treatments
- Weight tracker with a chart to spot trends over time
- Parasite prevention log: flea, tick, heartworm, deworming and testing
- Grooming log: baths, nail trims, ear cleaning, dental care
- Allergies and diet notes with a food change and reaction log
- Emergency info cards to cut out for your wallet, carrier and pet sitter
- Annual health summary

WHO IT'S FOR
- Dog and cat owners who want records ready for boarding, daycare, groomers and travel
- Pet parents managing a senior pet or an ongoing medication routine
- Multi-pet households that need to keep everyone's care straight
- New owners starting good habits from day one
