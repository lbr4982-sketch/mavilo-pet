Section 1

How to Use This Record Book

Good records help your veterinarian, your pet sitter and you make better decisions — especially in an emergency.

This book gathers your pet’s important health information in one place. Print one complete set per pet, keep it in a folder or binder, and bring it to every vet visit. Print extra copies of any log page as it fills up.

Tips for great records

Write it down right away

Fill in the log at the clinic or as soon as you get home, while details are fresh. Ask the clinic for a printed visit summary and staple it behind the log.

Copy exactly from the label

For medications, copy the drug name, strength and instructions exactly as written on the prescription label by your veterinarian. Never guess or change a dose.

Track trends

Weigh your pet monthly. Small, steady changes in weight, appetite, thirst or energy are easier to spot on paper — and can be early signs worth mentioning to your vet.

Keep copies

Snap photos of each completed page so you have a backup on your phone for travel, boarding and emergencies.

What to bring to every appointment

  • This record book
  • List of current medications and supplements
  • Food brand, amount and treats
  • Notes or videos of any symptoms
  • Fresh stool sample, if requested
  • Questions written down
  • Insurance information
  • Pet on leash or in a secure carrier
