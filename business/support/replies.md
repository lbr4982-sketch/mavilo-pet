# 고객 응대 정형 답변 (business/support/replies.md)

Etsy 메시지·Shopify 문의·리뷰 답글에 **복사해서 붙이는** 영어 답변 모음입니다. Etsy에는 메시지 API가 없으므로
사장님이 직접 붙여 넣습니다(주 5분). `[ ]` 안은 바꿔 넣는 자리입니다. 모든 답변은 `factory/COPY.md`의 문구와 일치합니다.

사용 원칙:
- 24시간 안에 답장(Etsy 별점 기준 "응답 속도"에 반영).
- 파일 문제는 **항상 먼저 해결**(재전송/수정)하고, 그래도 안 되면 환불. 파일 문제로 환불을 거절하지 않음.
- 건강 관련 질문에는 절대 개별 조언을 하지 않음 → "Is this vet advice?" 답변 사용.
- 비밀키·계정 정보·내부 문서 링크를 답장에 넣지 않음.

## 2. 파일이 안 열려요 (file won't open)

```
Hi [Name],

Sorry about that. Let's get it open.

The guide is a standard PDF. The most common fixes:
- On iPhone/iPad: download inside Safari (not the Etsy app), then open in the Files app or Books.
- On Android: open with Google Drive PDF viewer or Adobe Acrobat Reader (free).
- On a computer: open with Adobe Acrobat Reader, Preview (Mac) or Microsoft Edge/Chrome. If the file shows 0 KB, the download was interrupted; please download it again.
- Files zipped by Etsy: unzip first (right-click > Extract / tap the .zip).

If it still will not open, tell me your device and what you see on screen, and I will send a fresh copy within 24 hours. If we cannot get a working file to you, you will receive a full refund.

Warm regards,
Mavilo Pet Co.
Happy pets, practical care.
```

## 3. 인쇄 팁 (printing tips)

```
Hi [Name],

Great question! Here is how to get the best print:

- Paper: US Letter (8.5 x 11 in). In the print dialog choose "Fit to page" or "Scale: 100%".
- On A4 paper choose "Fit to printable area"; the margins are wide enough that nothing is cut off.
- Colour: the guide prints well in black and white; use colour for the cover and charts if you like.
- Trackers and logs are designed to be printed as many times as you need. Print just those pages by entering the page numbers (for example "12-14").
- Print shops (Staples, FedEx Office, Office Depot) accept the PDF as-is; ask for "letter, single-sided, colour or B&W".

Happy printing, and give [pet's name] a scratch from us!

Warm regards,
Mavilo Pet Co.
Happy pets, practical care.
```

[Etsy: The order shows "Download files" as available on the buyer's Purchases page. / Shopify: The download link was delivered to [email] at [time]; our records show the file was downloaded [N] time(s) at [time(s)].]

Description: The listing clearly states "Instant digital download (PDF). Nothing will be shipped." and our refund policy ("all sales are final for digital downloads; file problems are fixed or refunded within 30 days") is shown in the listing and shop policies.

Buyer contact: [The buyer did not contact us before opening this dispute. / The buyer messaged on [date]; we replied within [N] hours offering [fresh file / refund]; see attached.]

Evidence attached: (1) listing screenshot with digital-download notice and policy, (2) order and download record, (3) message history.

Mavilo Pet Co.

## 10. Etsy 정책 위반 통지 항소 초안 (policy notice appeal, structure)

Etsy에서 "Creativity Standards" 또는 AI 관련 삭제 통지가 왔을 때. 통지의 정확한 문구와 리스팅 ID를 먼저 적어 두고, AI가 이 구조로 영문 초안을 작성 → 사장님이 검토 후 제출. **항소는 1회만**(설계서 9장). 제출 전에 유사 리스팅을 점검하고 `PAUSE`를 확인.

```
Subject: Appeal, listing [ID] "[title]", notice dated [date]

1. Who we are (2 sentences)
   Mavilo Pet Co. is a one-person shop based in [country] selling printable pet-care guides. I personally direct, review and approve every guide before it is listed.

2. What the listing is (2-3 sentences)
   [Title] is an original [N]-page printable PDF: [one line on the structure, e.g. a 4-week schedule, tracker pages and a checklist] written for U.S. pet owners. It is not a template purchased elsewhere and is not a re-skin of another seller's design.

3. How it meets the Creativity Standards (bullets, each with evidence)
   - Original creative direction: the outline, page structure and text were specified by me [attach the brief / outline file with dates].
   - AI use disclosed: the description states "[AI_DISCLOSURE sentence verbatim]" [attach screenshot].
   - Human review: each guide is reviewed and approved by me before listing [attach review checklist / PR approval screenshot without internal URLs].
   - Own design system: all guides share our own colour palette, cover layout and page templates created for this shop [attach cover set image].

4. What we changed (if anything)
   [Either "No changes were needed; the listing already complied" or the concrete edits: added disclosure to line 1 of the description, replaced image N, removed phrase X.]

5. Request (1-2 sentences)
   I respectfully ask that the listing be reinstated. I am happy to provide the source files or answer any question about how the guide was created.

Thank you for your time.
[Name], Mavilo Pet Co.
```
